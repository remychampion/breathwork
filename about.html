Meet Remy Champion A certified breathwork practitioner with experience working 1-1, with groups, and in corporate spaces. The breath is a tool available to all of us to alter our state of being, and I’m here to guide you on that journey. Remy Champion
My Background Sports, Tech, and Alternative Therapies
I spent my youth playing water polo and swimming Born and raised in Hawaii, I grew up in the pool and ocean. Water polo took me from UC Berkeley (go Bears!) to a professional team in Madrid, and taught me early that breath and the body are performance tools, but they need maintenance.

Then I spent 15 years in tech, and 10 of them in cybersecurity. I lived in my head, hustled hard, and burned out often, with few tools that truly helped me to regulate. I know what sustained pressure does to a nervous system because I lived it.

And of course, life doesn’t pause while you you’re trying to do your best at work or in relationships. Painful, difficult things happen while you’re still showing up to meetings and hitting deadlines. In 2020, a ski accident left me with a concussion that took five years to recover from. I tried every therapy people recommended and learned how much trauma the body holds, and how it can heal with the right guidance.

After the Maui fires, I found transformational breathwork. It helped me process hard experiences that were living in my body, without losing my center. It brought me out of my head and back to my heart, my body, and myself. Performing better at work was a side effect. The point was feeling resilient and grounded.

I teach breathwork because it’s a powerful somatic tool to help alter mental, physical and emotional states - and I’ve experienced this firsthand. I help busy, high-performing people step out of constant thinking and back into their bodies, so they can lead and live with more steadiness.
