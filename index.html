The practice Transformational breathwork
An active style of breathwork to connect with our authentic selves and human gifts. This style of breathwork loads our body with oxygen, and ironically gives us something to do while we let go. Through intentional active breathing, we make room for awareness, release, and a deeper sense of connection with ourselves.
